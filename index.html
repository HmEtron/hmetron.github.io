How it works

Grade Calculator uses your assessment marks and their weightings to calculate your current overall grade. You can also enter your target grade to find out what average mark you need on your remaining assessments, or calculate the exact mark you need on a final assessment to reach your goal. Simply enter your assessment results, add any remaining assessments, and let the calculator do the maths for you.

Example

Imagain you have two assessments, you enter in one for 80% grade and the other for a 60% grade. Weightings 40% and 30%. The overall grade would be a 75.71% or B.
If you then entered in a 75% target, it would tell you that you need an average of 73.3 across your remaining asessments.
Then for the final grade you put in 80% target and 30% final weighing. It would tell you that you need a 90% grade to acheve the 80%.

FAQ

What is a weighted grade?
A weighted grade takes into account how much each assessment contributes to your overall result. An assessment worth 40% will have a greater impact on your final grade than one worth 10%.
Can I use this calculator for different subjects?**
Yes. You can use Grade Calculator for any subject that uses marks and assessment weightings.
What if I want to reach a specific grade?**
Enter your target percentage in the Target Grade section. The calculator will work out the average mark you need across your remaining assessments.
Can I find out what I need on my final assessment?**
Yes. Enter your target grade and the weighting of your final assessment, and the calculator will calculate the mark you need.
What happens if my target is impossible?**
If your target cannot be reached based on the assessments you have entered, the calculator will let you know that the target is unachievable.
